# Job Hunter: how to use it

## Every day (about 10 minutes)
1. Open https://job-hunter-teriekarie-8558.vercel.app and sign in (same email and password as your content manager app).
2. The **daily search** has already run at about 6:00am Lagos time. New jobs are in the **New** tab, best match first.
   To search again any time, click **Find new jobs now** (takes about a minute).
3. For each job, read the card. Click **View full details** for everything: salary, who can apply and why,
   the legit check with evidence, why it fits, gaps, and the full description.
4. Decide:
   - **Apply on site ↗**: opens the employer's page. When you come back, click **Yes, mark as Applied**.
   - **Prepare email application** (jobs that ask for email): check To / Subject / Message, fill anything in
     [square brackets], click **Open in Gmail**, attach your CV and cover letter, send, then click **I've sent it: mark as Applied**.
   - **Cover letter**: written the first time you open it. Edit, save, copy or download it.
   - **Shortlist**: keep for later.
   - **Not a match**: pick the reasons. They show on Profile & rules so you can turn them into rules.
5. When a company replies, open the job and change the **Stage** (Acknowledged, Interviewing, Offer, Rejected...).

## Jobs from LinkedIn, Indeed, Jobberman, Jobgurus, Google
Use the **Search other sites yourself** buttons at the bottom of the Jobs page. When you find a job, click
**+ Add a job I found**, paste the link, title, company, location and the job text. It is checked and scored like the others.

## Keeping AI costs low
- Without an AI key the app is free to run (rules and templates).
- With a key, AI is only used when you click: **Cover letter**, **Prepare email**, or **Check fit with AI** on a job (about 1–3 US cents each).
- On **Profile & rules** you can switch on AI scoring for every search (about 1–3 cents a day) and set a **monthly limit** (default $3). When the limit is reached, the app goes back to rules until next month.

## Weekly
- **Tracker**: everything you have applied to. A "Time to follow up" badge appears after 7 days with no reply,
  with a ready follow-up email. **Download as spreadsheet** exports it.
- **Search history**: what each search found, and every dropped job with the reason. If a good job was dropped by mistake, add it yourself.
- **Profile & rules**: update your CV text when anything changes (letters only use what is written there), add words to skip, edit what the app has learned.

## Labels explained
- **Worldwide / Nigeria eligible / Africa eligible**: you can apply from Lagos.
- **EMEA**: Europe, Middle East and Africa. Usually fine, but check Nigeria is not excluded.
- **Check who can apply**: the posting does not say. Read the evidence before applying.
- **Legit check: verified** = on 2+ boards or the employer's own hiring page. **Needs review** = one board only (normal for small companies). **High risk** jobs (fees, Telegram-only, ID requests) are never shown.
- **Score**: Excellent 85+, Strong 70–84, Potential 55–69, Stretch below 55.
